Point at the travel drive/export folder (the same one you exported to, e.g. F:\TravelMedia) and import everything that changed in one shot - watched marks, retention changes, new files found via Scan New Downloads, pinned banner images. Nothing here is ever auto-deleted; a title flagged "delete after watching" out there is only reported, never removed from your library automatically.

Picked up something new on this trip? Nothing is imported into your home library until you bring this drive back and load the saved manifest there.
